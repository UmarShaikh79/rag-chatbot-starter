# Sharing and collaboration

## Sharing a notebook

Open a notebook, choose Share, and enter the email addresses of the people you want to invite. Each person gets one of three roles: Viewer (read only), Commenter (read and comment) or Editor (read, comment and edit).

A notebook can be shared with up to 100 people. Only verified accounts can share notebooks.

## Public links

Plus and Team users can publish a single note as a read-only public link. Anyone with the link can view the note without signing in. You can turn the link off at any time, and links can be set to expire after 1, 7 or 30 days.

## Real-time editing

Editors see each other's cursors and changes live. Up to 25 people can edit the same note at once.

## Comments

Commenters and Editors can leave comments on any paragraph. Mention someone with @name to notify them by email.
