Privacy and your data

This page explains how Tidepool Notes, a fictional product, stores and handles your data.

Where data is stored

Notes are stored in data centers in the European Union (Frankfurt) by default. Team plans can choose United States (Oregon) storage when the team space is created; the region cannot be changed later.

Encryption

All notes are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Plus and Team users can turn on end-to-end encryption for individual notebooks; Tidepool staff cannot read end-to-end encrypted notebooks, and they cannot be recovered if you lose your passphrase.

Exporting your data

You can export all notes at any time from Settings > Account > Export. Exports are ZIP files with Markdown notes and the original attachments.

Deleting your account

  • Deleted notes stay in the Trash for 30 days before they are removed permanently.
  • When you delete your account, all notes are removed from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.
