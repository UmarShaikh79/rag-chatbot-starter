# Plans and billing

## Plans

Tidepool Notes has three plans.

- **Free**: up to 2 devices, 60 MB of uploads per month, and 7 days of note history.
- **Plus**: costs 4 US dollars per month or 40 US dollars per year. Unlimited devices, 5 GB of uploads per month, and 1 year of note history.
- **Team**: costs 8 US dollars per user per month, billed annually. Includes everything in Plus, shared team spaces, admin controls and single sign-on (SSO).

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Team plans can also pay by bank transfer when they have 20 or more seats.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade at any time and the new features are available immediately. The price difference is prorated for the rest of the billing period. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the account owner and are also available under Settings > Billing > Invoices. To add a company name or VAT number, edit the billing profile before the next invoice is issued; past invoices cannot be changed.
