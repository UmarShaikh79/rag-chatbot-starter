# Refund policy

## Annual plans

Annual Plus and Team subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you can cancel to stop the next renewal.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions are not refunded for partial months. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you already paid for.

## How to request a refund

Go to Settings > Billing and choose Request refund, or contact support with your invoice number. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Exceptions

If you were charged twice by mistake, or charged after cancelling, we refund the extra charge regardless of the 30-day window.
